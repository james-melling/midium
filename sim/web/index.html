Power & battery

76%

Hold the joystick press for 2 s anywhere for the power-off countdown, 2 s more to power off; release to cancel. With the controller fitted the power is cut; without it (the breadboard) the box goes to soft-off. Skip idle time jumps to 50 s before auto-off. Charging is sped up to 0.5 %/s. The gauge and strap options apply at the next power-up.

Inputs

Each button sends real traffic into IN A or IN B at MIDI wire speed: 31,250 baud, 320 µs per byte.

Outputs

Every byte the engine sends, timestamped as it starts on the wire, grouped into messages. Newest first.

noteCC / bend / aftertouchprogram / system common sysex / MSC / MTC fullrealtime / quarter frame

Default routing is the firmware default: IN A goes to both outputs and IN B is monitored only. Change it on the device under Menu > Routing. Settings live in RAM, as on the hardware so far, so a power cycle or display change resets them (including the power settings). The clock starts from this computer's time, and the flash log (emulated in RAM with the real format code) survives power cycles until the page is reloaded.
